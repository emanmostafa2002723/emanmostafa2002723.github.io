M.Sc. Graduation Project

NORA — Mental Health Conversational AI

Queen's University · team of four
  • Fine-tuned a DeBERTa-v3 crisis classifier (binary gate plus five categories): 96.86% crisis recall (401 of 414 posts) and 81.59% macro-F1 on a 600-post clinician-annotated test set.
  • Cut false alarms from 14 to 2 and crisis misses from 3 to 1 on 60 authored cases versus the earlier baseline.
  • Built the boundary classifier and rules guard separating restricted diagnosis, medication, and treatment requests from general education: 58 of 60 exact matches, and 38 of 40 on a contrast suite.
  • Developed the response-policy layer and route resolution, led prompt construction and complete-response buffering, and co-developed the output guardrail that checks full answers before display.

Individual contributions, excluding the core generative model and RAG pipeline.
